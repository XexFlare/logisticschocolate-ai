# Logistics KPI Guide

Key logistics performance indicators include shipment count, delivered volume, revenue per route, average delay hours, cancellation rate, utilization, and tonnage moved.

Revenue by route helps identify profitable corridors and high-volume destinations. Tonnage trends show operational demand over time and can support planning for fleet allocation, depot readiness, and customer demand.

Delay hours should be interpreted carefully. Some delays are caused by route conditions, border clearance, loading delays, documentation gaps, weather, or mechanical issues. A route with high revenue and high delay may still be commercially important, but it requires operational attention.
